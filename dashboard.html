What It Predicts
The model is optimized against two simultaneous objectives — emission share capture and alpha price appreciation in TAO terms. Both matter to a real staker. Emission yield compounds with price appreciation multiplicatively; optimizing for one while ignoring the other leaves returns on the table.
No Look-Ahead Bias
