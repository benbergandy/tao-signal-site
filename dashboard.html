Dual Objective
The model is optimized against two simultaneous objectives — emission share capture and alpha price appreciation in TAO terms. Both matter to a real staker. Emission yield and price appreciation compound multiplicatively.
No Look-Ahead Bias
Model weights were finalized on a training period before the validation window. The held-out test set was never touched during development. Live positions are locked using only data available at signal time.
