📐 Methodology
Return Framework
Staker returns in Bittensor are multiplicative across two dimensions: emission yield and alpha price appreciation. A position captures a share of network emissions proportional to its weight, while the exchange rate between alpha and TAO fluctuates independently. Total return compounds both effects simultaneously — optimizing for either dimension in isolation leaves returns on the table.

Total return formula (1+e) × (1+p) − 1
Model Portfolios
Three portfolios are generated by the model, each reflecting a different risk/return profile. Established targets proven high-emission subnets with validated momentum — the lower-risk allocation. Balanced is the model's primary recommendation, blending signal strength with volatility adjustment. Speculative concentrates in the highest-conviction mean-reversion candidates — higher variance, higher potential upside.

Benchmark Construction
Two benchmarks are tracked against the model portfolios. The Large Cap Index holds the ten highest-emission subnets at equal weight — the naive strategy most stakers default to. The Network Average holds all active subnets at equal weight, representing a fully passive, undifferentiated allocation. Outperforming both benchmarks across multiple evaluation windows is the validation standard.
Evaluation Standards
Each daily position snapshot is evaluated at fixed forward windows: 7, 14, 30, 60, and 90 days. Positions are locked at snapshot time using only information available at that moment — no look-ahead, no rebalancing within a window. Statistical significance requires a minimum of 30 completed periods. Early results are indicative; conclusions are drawn at 60–90 days of data.
