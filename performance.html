📐 Methodology
How the Track Record Works
Three Portfolios
TAO Signal Combined — top 5 by our composite score (the signal we're validating).

Top Emission — top 5 by raw emission share (Crucible-style benchmark).

Top Flow — top 5 by EMA TAO inflow (momentum benchmark).
How Returns Are Measured
Returns are measured as average emission share captured by each portfolio — the % of total network emissions flowing to held subnets.

Higher emission share = more TAO flowing to your staked position. This is the cleanest measure of staking return independent of alpha price volatility.
Evaluation Windows
Each daily snapshot locks in positions for three forward windows: 7, 14, and 30 days.

At each window close, the portfolio's average emission share is compared to the network average (1/128 = 0.78%). Outperformance is expressed as a multiple of the baseline.
No Look-Ahead Bias
Positions are locked at the time of snapshot using only data available at that moment. No rebalancing occurs within a window.

The cron job runs at 8am EDT every day. All scores and positions are determined by that morning's chain data only.
