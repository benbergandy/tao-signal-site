📐 Methodology
How the Track Record Works
Three Return Metrics
Emission share — the % of total network TAO emissions flowing to held subnets. Higher = more TAO yield from staking.

Alpha price return — change in each subnet's alpha token price in TAO terms since entry. TAO-denominated to remove USD volatility.

Total return — multiplicative combination: (1 + emission_return) × (1 + price_return) − 1. This is what a real staker earns.
No Look-Ahead Bias
Positions locked using only data available at snapshot time. No rebalancing within a window.

Entry price recorded as moving_price (EMA of alpha/TAO) at the moment of lock — the right reference price for TAO-denominated returns, smoothed against intraday noise.

Cron runs at 8am EDT. All scores determined by that morning's chain data only.
Benchmarks
Top Emission — top 5 by raw emission share. The "just buy the biggest subnets" strategy most naive stakers follow.

Top Flow — top 5 by EMA TAO inflow. A momentum benchmark.

Network Average — equal weight across all active subnets. The passive baseline.
Evaluation Windows
Each daily snapshot is evaluated at 7, 14, 30, 60, and 90 day windows.

At each close, all three return metrics are compared against the network average baseline.

First 7-day results: April 2, 2026. Full 30-day picture: April 25. Statistical significance requires 60-90 days of data.
