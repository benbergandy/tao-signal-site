IMPORTANT: AUTOMATED PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT
By enabling the portfolio manager, you authorize the TAO Signals bot to automatically stake and unstake TAO on your behalf according to your configured strategy. This means:
  • Your current positions may be rebalanced or exited
  • New positions may be opened in subnets selected by the model

  • You can disable the bot at any time — all positions remain yours
  • Your private keys never leave your device — the bot signs locally
